# POLITICAL VISUAL IDENTITY

**Version:** 1.0  
**Purpose:** Visual Knowledge Base for Maknaflow  
**Content Type:** Faceless Political / Social / Economic Content  
**Primary Format:** Vertical Video 9:16

---

# 1. VISUAL IDENTITY

## Core Identity

> **Editorial Graphic Novel × Isometric Society × Symbolic Metaphor**

Channel menggunakan dunia visual editorial yang menggambarkan politik, ekonomi, masyarakat, dan sistem melalui karakter faceless, lingkungan ilustratif, serta metafora visual.

Visual tidak boleh terasa seperti:

- stock footage
- generic AI cinematic video
- food/lifestyle content
- talking-head podcast
- news TV
- realistic political documentary

Target utama:

> **Penonton harus dapat mengenali channel hanya dari visualnya, bahkan tanpa melihat logo atau nama akun.**

---

# 2. VISUAL PRINCIPLES

Semua visual mengikuti 6 prinsip:

### 2.1 Faceless

Karakter manusia tidak memiliki detail wajah.

Tidak menampilkan:

- mata detail
- ekspresi wajah realistis
- lip-sync
- close-up wajah

Emosi disampaikan melalui:

- body language
- pose
- framing
- lighting
- environment
- scale

---

### 2.2 Editorial

Setiap frame harus terasa seperti ilustrasi dari:

- editorial magazine
- political essay
- graphic novel
- visual journalism

Bukan sekadar gambar yang indah.

Visual harus membantu menjelaskan narasi.

---

### 2.3 System-Oriented

Politik tidak selalu divisualisasikan sebagai politisi.

Gunakan representasi sistem seperti:

- masyarakat
- institusi
- perusahaan
- pemerintah
- pasar
- bank
- media
- rumah tangga
- infrastruktur
- arus uang
- jaringan kekuasaan

Visual harus mampu menunjukkan hubungan antaraktor.

---

### 2.4 Symbolic

Konsep abstrak harus sebisa mungkin diterjemahkan menjadi objek atau kejadian visual.

Contoh:

Inflasi  
→ keranjang belanja membesar sementara uang mengecil.

Utang  
→ kota berdiri di atas tumpukan tagihan.

Ketimpangan  
→ dua lift bergerak dengan kecepatan berbeda.

Kekuasaan  
→ satu kursi sangat besar dikelilingi kursi-kursi kecil.

Beban ekonomi  
→ karakter membawa tumpukan balok semakin tinggi.

---

### 2.5 Minimal

Hindari frame terlalu ramai.

Setiap scene memiliki:

**1 primary subject + 1 primary idea.**

Elemen lain hanya mendukung pesan utama.

---

### 2.6 Visual Continuity

Scene dalam satu video harus terasa berasal dari universe yang sama.

Pertahankan:

- character design
- material
- lighting
- texture
- perspective
- visual density
- environment style

---

# 3. THREE VISUAL MODES

Maknaflow menggunakan tiga mode visual utama.

---

## MODE A — SYMBOLIC METAPHOR

### Function

Digunakan untuk:

- hook
- konsep abstrak
- konflik
- turning point
- kesimpulan

### Visual Characteristics

- surreal
- symbolic
- minimal
- dramatic scale
- strong visual contrast
- single dominant idea

### Examples

Narasi:

> Harga kebutuhan terus meningkat.

Visual:

> A faceless worker holding a small wallet while a giant grocery basket towers above him.

Narasi:

> Kekuasaan semakin terkonsentrasi.

Visual:

> Hundreds of small chairs slowly sliding toward one enormous chair in the center.

Narasi:

> Masyarakat menanggung bebannya.

Visual:

> A faceless citizen walking while carrying an increasingly tall stack of concrete blocks.

### Rule

**Do not illustrate the sentence literally when a stronger visual metaphor is possible.**

---

# 4. MODE B — EDITORIAL GRAPHIC NOVEL

### Function

Mode utama untuk storytelling.

Digunakan untuk:

- masyarakat
- aktivitas manusia
- konflik sosial
- historical reconstruction
- kehidupan sehari-hari
- cause and effect
- human impact

### Character Style

Faceless semi-realistic human.

Body proportion:

- realistic
- slightly stylized
- clean silhouette

Clothing:

- contemporary
- simple
- neutral
- context appropriate

Avoid:

- exaggerated fashion
- fantasy costume
- excessive accessories

### Environment

Contoh:

- city street
- office
- market
- factory
- home
- school
- public transportation
- government building
- meeting room
- neighborhood

### Rendering

Visual harus menyerupai:

**premium editorial illustration + modern graphic novel**

Gunakan:

- simplified geometry
- strong shadow
- subtle paper texture
- controlled detail
- cinematic composition

---

# 5. MODE C — ISOMETRIC SOCIETY

### Function

Digunakan ketika narasi menjelaskan:

- sistem
- struktur
- hubungan
- aliran
- proses
- jaringan
- sebab-akibat kompleks

### Visual Concept

Masyarakat digambarkan seperti miniature system.

Contoh elemen:

- houses
- factories
- banks
- markets
- government buildings
- farms
- ports
- roads
- schools
- hospitals
- media buildings

### Example

Narasi:

> Kenaikan harga tidak selalu dimulai dari pasar.

Visual sequence:

Farm  
↓  
Distributor  
↓  
Transport  
↓  
Market  
↓  
Consumer

Kamera bergerak mengikuti alur tersebut dalam satu dunia isometric.

### Important Rule

Isometric mode bukan sekadar aesthetic.

Mode ini hanya digunakan ketika:

> **Spatial relationships help explain the system.**

Maknaflow menentukan visual berdasarkan fungsi narasi.

| Narrative Function | Preferred Mode |

| Hook | Symbolic Metaphor |

| Institutional relationship | Isometric Society |
| Flow of money/resources | Isometric Society |

# 7. CHARACTER SYSTEM

## Primary Human

Default character:

> **Faceless semi-realistic contemporary human**

Characteristics:

- no facial features
- realistic body proportion
- simplified clothing
- recognizable silhouette
- restrained body language

---

## Citizen

Visual archetype:

- worker
- parent
- student
- merchant
- commuter
- farmer
- office worker

Never stereotype social groups.

---

## Authority / Institution

Represent through:

- silhouette
- position
- environment
- architecture
- desk
- podium
- meeting room

Avoid automatically portraying authority figures as:

- evil
- sinister
- threatening
- heroic

Visual framing should follow the factual role in the narrative.

---

## Crowd

Crowds use simplified faceless characters.

Individual facial identity is unnecessary.

Use crowd scale to communicate:

- participation
- public impact
- social scale
- collective behavior

Not ideological judgment.

---

# 8. COLOR SYSTEM

Use a restrained editorial palette.

## Base

- charcoal
- off-white
- warm gray
- muted beige

## Supporting Colors

Use restrained:

- muted red
- muted blue
- muted amber
- muted green

Avoid:

- neon palette
- rainbow gradients
- glossy commercial colors
- excessive saturation

### Rule

Color should communicate hierarchy, not decoration.

One scene should normally contain:

**1 dominant base palette + maximum 1 accent color.**

---

# 9. LIGHTING

Default lighting:

> **Soft directional editorial lighting with strong readable shadows.**

Use shadows to create:

- depth
- hierarchy
- separation
- tension

Avoid:

- excessive lens flare
- cyberpunk lighting
- glossy commercial lighting
- unrealistic RGB lighting

---

# 10. TEXTURE

Signature texture:

> **Subtle printed-paper grain + editorial ink texture**

Texture should be visible enough to differentiate the visual identity but never obscure the image.

Avoid:

- hyper-clean 3D render
- plastic appearance
- excessive film grain
- heavy vintage filters

---

# 11. COMPOSITION

Default format:

**9:16 vertical**

Composition priorities:

1. primary subject
2. visual metaphor
3. environment
4. negative space

Maintain clear silhouette.

Avoid placing important subjects directly at:

- extreme top
- extreme bottom
- UI overlay zones

Whenever possible create depth using:

**Foreground → Subject → Background**

---

# 12. CAMERA LANGUAGE

Preferred shots:

- wide establishing
- medium
- medium-wide
- top-down
- isometric
- over-the-shoulder without visible face
- silhouette profile
- environmental wide shot

Limit:

- extreme close-up
- face close-up
- random drone shot
- excessive camera rotation

Camera movement:

- slow push-in
- pull-back reveal
- lateral tracking
- controlled pan
- top-down transition

Camera movement must reveal information.

Do not move the camera only for cinematic effect.

---

# 13. VISUAL HOOK RULE

First 1–3 seconds must contain:

**Visual anomaly + recognizable subject + unresolved question**

Example:

Normal:

> A worker shopping at a supermarket.

Better:

> A tiny faceless worker standing beneath a grocery basket ten times larger than his body.

Normal:

> Government building.

Better:

> Hundreds of roads converging into a single government building.

Normal:

> Money losing value.

Better:

> A banknote physically shrinking inside someone's hand.

### Rule

> **The first frame should create a question before the narration explains it.**

---

# 14. VISUAL METAPHOR ENGINE

For abstract concepts use:

**CONCEPT → PHYSICAL OBJECT → VISUAL ACTION**

Example:

Inflation

→ money + grocery basket

→ money shrinks while basket grows.

Power concentration

→ chairs

→ hundreds of chairs merge into one giant chair.

Economic burden

→ concrete blocks

→ blocks accumulate on citizen's back.

Information control

→ information pipes

→ many pipes converge through one narrow gate.

Inequality

→ elevators

→ one rises rapidly while another barely moves.

---

# 15. MOTION PRINCIPLES

Motion should communicate:

- transformation
- cause-effect
- scale
- relationship
- reveal

Preferred motion:

- object growing
- object shrinking
- objects converging
- objects separating
- flow moving
- structure assembling
- structure collapsing
- camera revealing larger system

Avoid meaningless motion.

---

# 16. SCENE TRANSITION

Preferred transitions should be object-driven.

Examples:

coin  
→ becomes building

road  
→ becomes graph line

document  
→ becomes city

shopping basket  
→ becomes economic diagram

factory smoke  
→ becomes chart

crowd  
→ becomes dots in an isometric system

Avoid excessive:

- glitch
- flash
- spin
- generic zoom transition

---

# 17. TYPOGRAPHY

Typography style:

**Modern Editorial Sans Serif**

Characteristics:

- bold
- condensed when appropriate
- high readability
- minimal decoration

On-screen text should primarily show:

- key number
- key term
- year
- short question
- short statement

Avoid paragraphs inside video.

Maximum recommended:

**3–7 words per text element.**

---

# 18. DATA VISUALIZATION

When numbers appear, convert them into visual scale whenever possible.

Instead of:

> 70%

Use:

> 7 of 10 visual units highlighted.

Instead of:

> Rp100 trillion

Use proportional stacks or blocks when the comparison is meaningful and sourced.

Charts should remain:

- simple
- readable
- proportional
- contextually labeled

Never manipulate visual scale to exaggerate political claims.

---

# 19. POLITICAL FIGURE REPRESENTATION

When a specific public figure is necessary:

Prefer:

- recognizable silhouette where appropriate
- contextual environment
- podium
- institutional setting
- restrained editorial representation

Do not use visual manipulation intended to make a person appear:

- evil
- heroic
- stupid
- frightening
- saint-like

Visual representation should support explanation rather than judgment.

---

# 20. FORBIDDEN VISUAL PATTERNS

Avoid:

### Generic AI Cinematic

> realistic man walking through cinematic city, dramatic lighting, shallow depth of field

### Generic News

> politician podium + parliament + flag + random crowd

### Generic Podcast

> two people sitting across microphones

### Generic Corporate Infographic

> floating icons + blue gradient + corporate character

### Food/Lifestyle Aesthetic

Avoid visual grammar associated with other Maknaflow brands:

- macro food photography
- kitchen scenes
- hands preparing ingredients
- bright lifestyle photography
- product beauty shots

---

# 21. DEFAULT SCENE STRUCTURE

For short-form political content:

### Scene 01 — Hook

**Symbolic Metaphor**

Create visual anomaly.

### Scene 02 — Context

**Graphic Novel**

Show human or event context.

### Scene 03 — Mechanism

**Isometric Society**

Explain how the system works.

### Scene 04 — Consequence

**Graphic Novel**

Show impact on people.

### Scene 05 — Reveal

**Isometric / Symbolic**

Reveal larger relationship.

### Scene 06 — Conclusion

**Symbolic Metaphor**

End with one memorable visual.

This structure is a default, not a mandatory template.

# 25. CONSISTENCY RULE

Every generated scene must answer three questions:

### 1. What is the ONE idea being communicated?

If unclear → simplify scene.

### 2. Which visual mode explains it best?

Choose:

**SYMBOLIC / GRAPHIC NOVEL / ISOMETRIC**

### 3. Does the frame belong to the same visual universe?

Check:

- faceless characters
- editorial rendering
- restrained palette
- geometric shadows
- paper grain
- controlled composition

If not → regenerate.
