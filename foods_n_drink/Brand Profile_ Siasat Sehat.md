# **BRAND PROFILE: SIASAT SEHAT**

**Document Type:** Brand Data Variable

**System Integration Note:** This data is to be injected into KB-01: Core Brand Guidelines when processing requests for this specific client/brand.

## **1\. Brand Identity**

* **Brand Name:** Siasat Sehat  
* **Niche/Category:** Health & Wellness, Practical Cooking, Budget Lifestyle.

## **2\. Brand Vision & Mission**

* **Vision:** To make healthy living highly accessible, extremely practical, and highly affordable for the everyday Indonesian.  
* **Mission:** To prove that eating healthy does not require expensive imported ingredients, hours of cooking, or luxurious kitchen gadgets.

## **3\. Target Audience Profile**

* **Demographics:** Indonesians, aged 35 and above (35+).  
* **Psychographics:** Busy professionals, parents, or individuals who realize the importance of health but are easily intimidated by complex diets, calorie-counting, or time-consuming meal preps.  
* **Pain Points:** Lack of time, tight budgets, and the misconception that "healthy food is tasteless and expensive."  
* **Desires:** Simple, realistic, and delicious healthy alternatives that fit into a hectic daily routine.

## **4\. Tone of Voice (ToV) & Copywriting Style**

* **Persona:** Friendly & Approachable (like a helpful neighbor or a practical friend).  
* **Approach:** Solution-Oriented (always emphasize how easy and fast the solution is) and Down-to-Earth / *Merakyat*.  
* **Language Rules:**  
  * Use natural, everyday, yet polite Indonesian language.  
  * Consistently use pronouns like "Anda" (more formal/respectful) or "Kamu" (closer/friendlier) based on the specific campaign, but stick to one per video.  
  * Avoid heavy "Gen-Z" slang, overly academic nutrition jargon, or patronizing/preachy tones.

## **5\. Specific Brand Constraints (Do's & Don'ts)**

* **DO \- Local First:** Always prioritize ingredients that can be easily bought in Indonesian traditional markets (*pasar tradisional*) or local convenience stores (*minimarket* like Indomaret/Alfamart).  
* **DO \- Practicality:** Highlight the speed and ease of the cooking process.  
* **DON'T \- No Extreme Diets:** Never promote starvation, extreme calorie deficits, or restrictive fad diets.  
* **DON'T \- No Elitism in Ingredients:** Never use expensive, hard-to-find imported ingredients (e.g., Himalayan pink salt, rare berries) unless a cheap, local substitute is explicitly stated as the primary option.  
* **DON'T \- No Luxurious Equipment:** Avoid scenarios that require highly expensive, professional-grade kitchen appliances. Keep it to tools accessible to the middle class.
