# **FOODS & DRINKS \- KB-05: SIGNATURE MOMENTS & VISUAL DNA LIBRARY**

**Project:** Siasat Sehat \- Facebook Reels Production System

**Document Type:** Visual Hook & Retention Asset Library

## **1\. Document Function**

This document serves as the "Visual DNA" of the Siasat Sehat brand. It contains a curated library of highly satisfying, retention-boosting visual actions known as "Signature Moments." The AI must use this library to ensure every generated video has a distinct, memorable, and visually addictive quality that keeps viewers watching and sharing.

## **2\. The "Signature Moment" Mandate**

* **The Rule:** Every single video generated MUST include at least ONE Signature Moment.  
* **Placement:** The chosen Signature Moment must be strategically placed in either **Module A (Hook)** to stop the scroll, or **Module E (Beauty Shot)** to drive saves and shares.  
* **Execution:** The prompt for a Signature Moment must always be generated in slow motion or extreme close-up (Macro) to maximize the visual satisfaction.

## **3\. Signature Moment Library (Categorized)**

The AI must select an appropriate action from the categories below based on the recipe's core ingredients.

### **Category A: Liquid Dynamics (The Pour & Drizzle)**

*Best for: Smoothies, herbal drinks, salads, desserts.*

* **The Golden Drizzle:** Thick, viscous liquids (like pure honey, low-sugar syrup, or olive oil) drizzling slowly over textured food.  
* **The Perfect Vortex:** A top-down or side view of a blender creating a perfectly smooth, powerful liquid vortex.  
* **The Cloud Mix:** Pouring milk (or dairy-free alternatives) into dark coffee or dark tea, showing the beautiful, cloudy mixing process.  
* **The Splash Drop:** Dropping a large, crystal-clear ice cube into a glass of herbal drink, capturing the slow-motion splash.

### **Category B: Texture Reveals (The Cut & Pull)**

*Best for: Baked goods, sandwiches, meats, thick snacks.*

* **The Clean Slice:** A sharp knife slicing cleanly through a layered ingredient (e.g., an avocado, a stuffed tempeh, or a hard-boiled egg) to reveal the perfect interior.  
* **The Juicy Press:** Gently pressing a cooked protein (like chicken breast) with a fork to show the juices flowing out, proving it is not dry.  
* **The Soft Pull:** Tearing apart a piece of food (like healthy bread or a snack) to emphasize its soft, fluffy, or gooey interior.

### **Category C: Action & Physics (The Sprinkle & Steam)**

*Best for: Soups, stir-fries, oatmeals.*

* **The Majestic Steam:** Thick, warm steam rolling off a freshly cooked bowl of soup or a hot drink, heavily backlit by natural morning light to make the steam glow.  
* **The Slow Sprinkle:** Tiny ingredients (like chia seeds, toasted sesame seeds, chili flakes, or cinnamon powder) falling gently from above in extreme slow motion.  
* **The Sizzle Pan:** Dropping an ingredient into a hot pan and capturing the immediate, aggressive sizzle (combining visual oil bubbles and audio).

## **4\. Integration with Google Veo Prompts**

When the AI selects a Signature Moment from this library, the corresponding Google Veo prompt must include specific cinematic keywords to ensure the AI video generator focuses heavily on the action:

* **Required Keywords for this module:** extreme close up, macro lens, slow motion, hyper-detailed texture, satisfying visual, high frame rate (120fps style).
