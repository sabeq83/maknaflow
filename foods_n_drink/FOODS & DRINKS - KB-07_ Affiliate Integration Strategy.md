# **FOODS & DRINKS \- KB-07: AFFILIATE INTEGRATION STRATEGY**

**Project:** Siasat Sehat \- Facebook Reels Production System

**Document Type:** Monetization & Soft Selling Guide

## **1\. Document Function**

This document governs how the AI should integrate affiliate products into the video framework (specifically Module D). The goal is to drive high Click-Through Rates (CTR) and sales without triggering the viewer's "ad avoidance" reflex, thereby protecting the video's retention rate and algorithmic reach.

## **2\. The Core Philosophy: "Show, Don't Sell"**

* **The Rule:** The product must appear as a natural, helpful part of the cooking process, not as an interruption.  
* **The Approach:** Position the product as a "Problem Solver" or a "Time Saver." The viewer should want the product because they see how it makes the recipe easier or better.  
* **Strict Prohibition:** NEVER use hard-selling phrases in the Voice Over (e.g., "Buy now," "Click the link in my bio," "50% off today," "Get yours here").

## **3\. Pre-Requisite for Module D Inclusion**

Before generating Module D, the AI must ask: *"Does this recipe genuinely benefit from a specific tool or specialty ingredient?"*

* **Include Module D (Yes):** If the recipe requires blending (Blender), fine chopping (Chopper), oil-free frying (Air Fryer), or uses a specific healthy staple (Pure Honey, Rolled Oats, Chia Seeds).  
* **Skip Module D (No):** If the recipe only uses a standard knife, pan, and basic vegetables. Do not force a product where it doesn't belong.

## **4\. Affiliate Integration Methods (Visual & Audio)**

When an affiliate product is selected, the AI must use one of these integration strategies:

### **Strategy A: The "Texture / Quality" Proof**

* **Best for:** Blenders, Choppers, Grinders.  
* **Visual Strategy:** Show the raw ingredients going in, followed by a satisfying extreme close-up of the perfectly smooth/chopped result. Focus on the *output*, not just the machine.  
* **Voice Over Example:** "Untuk tekstur sehalus ini dalam hitungan detik, chopper berdaya tinggi ini sangat membantu." *(For a texture this smooth in seconds, this high-power chopper is very helpful.)*

### **Strategy B: The "Health / Guilt-Free" Enabler**

* **Best for:** Air Fryers, Non-stick pans (cooking with less oil).  
* **Visual Strategy:** Show the food cooking beautifully without swimming in oil. Focus on the crispiness or the clean pan.  
* **Voice Over Example:** "Biar tetap renyah tanpa banyak minyak goreng, saya pakai air fryer." *(To keep it crispy without a lot of cooking oil, I use an air fryer.)*

### **Strategy C: The "Hero Ingredient" Highlight**

* **Best for:** Pure Honey, Sea Salt, Premium Oats, Olive Oil.  
* **Visual Strategy:** A slow, satisfying "Signature Moment" (from KB-05) featuring the ingredient, such as a slow drizzle or a close-up sprinkle directly from its aesthetic packaging.  
* **Voice Over Example:** "Kunci rasa manis alaminya ada di madu murni ini." *(The key to its natural sweetness is this pure honey.)*

## **5\. Call to Action (CTA) Placement**

Since hard selling is banned in the main audio, the actual directive to purchase must be handled subtly in Module E (Beauty Shot) or in the caption.

* **Module E Voice Over (Soft CTA):** "Cek caption untuk resep, dan link alatnya ada di bio ya." *(Check the caption for the recipe, and the link for the tool is in the bio.)*  
* **Visual CTA:** A subtle text overlay during Module E (e.g., "Link Blender: No. 12 di Bio").

## **6\. Risk Mitigation (Retention Check)**

The AI must ensure that Module D does not exceed 8 seconds. It must feel like a seamless transition from Module C (Process). If the transition feels abrupt or "salesy," the AI must rewrite the module to blend better with the preceding cooking steps.
