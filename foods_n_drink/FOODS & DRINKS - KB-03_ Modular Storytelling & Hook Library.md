# **FOODS & DRINKS \- KB-03: MODULAR STORYTELLING & HOOK LIBRARY**

**Project:** Siasat Sehat \- Facebook Reels Production System

**Document Type:** Narrative Structure & Scripting Guide

## **1\. Document Function**

This document dictates the absolute narrative structure of every video. The AI must construct the video strictly using the defined 5-Module framework. It also provides a library of approved Hooks and CTAs (Call to Actions) to ensure high retention and engagement on Facebook Reels.

## **2\. The 5-Module Framework**

Every script and storyboard generated by the AI MUST follow this modular sequence. The total duration should fall between 32 to 40 seconds (each module is strictly 8 seconds).

### **Module A: HOOK (Mandatory \- 8 seconds)**

* **Goal:** Stop the scroll immediately within the first 3 seconds.  
* **Visual Strategy:** Start with the most visually appealing shot (e.g., the finished dish, a satisfying action like pouring or pulling).  
* **Audio Strategy:** Deliver the Hook phrase within the first 3 seconds.

### **Module B: INGREDIENT (Mandatory \- 8 seconds)**

* **Goal:** Establish that the recipe is incredibly simple and affordable.  
* **Visual Strategy:** Show the few ingredients clearly, often arranged aesthetically or being prepped rapidly.

### **Module C: PROCESS (Mandatory \- 8 seconds)**

* **Goal:** Prove that the cooking method is fast and uncomplicated.  
* **Visual Strategy:** Fast-paced cuts of the core cooking actions (stirring, blending, frying).

### **Module D: AFFILIATE INSERT (Optional \- 8 seconds)**

* **Goal:** Seamlessly integrate a product demonstration without breaking the viewer's immersion.  
* **Rule:** This module is ONLY included if the recipe genuinely benefits from a specific tool or ingredient. (Refer to KB-09 for detailed Affiliate rules). If omitted, the video proceeds directly to Module E.

### **Module E: BEAUTY SHOT & CTA (Mandatory \- 8 seconds)**

* **Goal:** Trigger cravings, establish final satisfaction, and drive viewer action (Save, Share, or Click).  
* **Visual Strategy:** Slow-motion, extreme close-up, or highly styled final presentation.  
* **Audio Strategy:** Deliver the chosen CTA clearly.

## **3\. Hook Library (For Module A)**

The AI must select or adapt a hook from these proven categories, ensuring it matches the recipe and the "Siasat Sehat" tone of voice.

### **Category 1: Problem-Solving & Empathy**

* "Perut sering begah setelah makan? Coba rutinin minum ini." *(Stomach often bloated? Try drinking this regularly.)*  
* "Pengen ngemil tapi takut timbangan naik? Bikin cemilan ini aja." *(Want to snack but scared of gaining weight? Make this instead.)*  
* "Sering lemes jam 10 pagi? Sarapan kamu kurang protein, coba menu ini." *(Often feel weak at 10 AM? Your breakfast lacks protein, try this menu.)*

### **Category 2: Curiosity & The "Secret"**

* "Ternyata bikin sarapan sehat cuma butuh 3 bahan pasar ini." *(Turns out making a healthy breakfast only takes these 3 market ingredients.)*  
* "Jangan buang pisang yang kematangan, ubah jadi cemilan diet seenak ini." *(Don't throw away overripe bananas, turn them into this delicious diet snack.)*  
* "Rahasia dada ayam diet tetep *juicy* dan nggak seret." *(The secret to juicy and non-dry diet chicken breast.)*

### **Category 3: Time & Cost Efficiency (High Priority)**

* "Menu diet tanggal tua, cuma modal 15 ribu bisa makan enak." *(End-of-month diet menu, only costs 15k to eat well.)*  
* "Cuma butuh 5 menit di pagi hari, kenyangnya sampai siang." *(Only takes 5 minutes in the morning, keeps you full until noon.)*  
* "Meal prep seminggu cuma modal 50 ribu, ini rahasianya." *(Weekly meal prep for only 50k, here is the secret.)*

## **4\. CTA (Call to Action) Library (For Module E)**

The AI must select a CTA that aligns with the specific goal of the video (Engagement or Sales).

### **Engagement & Save Focus (For non-affiliate videos)**

* "Jangan lupa di-save biar nggak lupa pas mau bikin." *(Don't forget to save so you don't forget when you want to make it.)*  
* "Share resep ini ke grup keluarga kamu." *(Share this recipe with your family group.)*  
* "Cek caption untuk takaran lengkapnya ya\!" *(Check the caption for full measurements\!)*

### **Affiliate & Conversion Focus (For videos with Module D)**

* "Alat yang ngebantu banget ini linknya ada di bio ya." *(The link to this very helpful tool is in the bio.)*  
* "Bahan utama resep ini udah aku taruh di keranjang kuning." *(I've put the main ingredient for this recipe in the yellow basket.)*  
* "Cek caption untuk resep, dan cek bio untuk panci anti lengketnya." *(Check caption for the recipe, and bio for the non-stick pan.)*

## **5\. Scripting Constraints**

* **Word Count:** Voice-over scripts must be concise. Aim for a maximum of 15-20 words per 8-second module to allow for pacing, breathing, and natural delivery.  
* **Visual-Audio Sync:** The Voice Over must always directly describe or complement the specific Visual happening in that exact module.
