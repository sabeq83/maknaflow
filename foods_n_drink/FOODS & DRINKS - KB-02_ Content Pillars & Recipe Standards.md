# **FOODS & DRINKS \- KB-02: CONTENT PILLARS & RECIPE STANDARDS**

**Project:** Siasat Sehat \- Facebook Reels Production System

**Document Type:** Production Standard & Recipe Logic

## **1\. Document Function**

This document governs the culinary logic of the AI. It ensures that every recipe generated for the Siasat Sehat brand falls strictly into approved content categories and adheres to absolute limits regarding complexity, time, and ingredient availability.

## **2\. Approved Content Pillars**

The AI must categorize every generated video into one of these four foundational pillars. Each pillar has a specific emotional target and purpose.

### **Pillar 1: Quick Breakfasts (Sarapan Sat Set)**

* **Purpose:** High-energy, nutritious meals to start the day, designed for busy mornings.  
* **Emotional Target:** "Wow, this is so fast and looks filling\!"  
* **Examples:** Oatmeal variations with local fruits, healthy egg wraps, quick tempeh sandwiches.

### **Pillar 2: Daily Detox & Herbal Drinks (Minuman Herbal/Detox Harian)**

* **Purpose:** Traditional-inspired or simple fruit/spice infusions for daily health, digestion, or immunity.  
* **Emotional Target:** "I have all these ingredients in my kitchen, I should make this now."  
* **Examples:** Ginger and lemongrass tea, turmeric honey shots, cucumber-lemon infused water.

### **Pillar 3: Guilt-Free Snacks (Cemilan Guilt-Free)**

* **Purpose:** Low-calorie or nutrient-dense alternatives to unhealthy junk food.  
* **Emotional Target:** "Finally, a snack I can eat without feeling guilty."  
* **Examples:** Air-fried cassava, baked sweet potato wedges, no-bake peanut butter oats.

### **Pillar 4: Budget Meal Prep (Meal Prep Murah)**

* **Purpose:** Lunch or dinner recipes that are affordable, protein-rich, and suitable for workers' lunchboxes.  
* **Emotional Target:** "This is so cheap but looks like a restaurant meal."  
* **Examples:** Grilled chicken breast with local spices, tofu/tempeh teriyaki, stir-fried vegetables.

## **3\. The "Siasat Sehat" Recipe Formula (Strict Limits)**

The AI MUST rigorously follow these quantitative limits for every recipe to maintain the brand's promise of "practicality":

* **Ingredient Limit:** Maximum of **7 main ingredients** (excluding basic water, standard cooking oil, salt, and pepper).  
* **Step Limit:** Maximum of **4 actionable cooking steps**.  
* **Active Time Limit:** The total active preparation and cooking time must realistically be **under 15 minutes** (passive time like boiling or baking can be longer, but must be specified).  
* **Equipment Constraint:** Recipes must primarily use standard equipment (stove, pan, blender). If an air fryer, chopper, or specific tool is required, it must be explicitly noted (and is a prime candidate for an Affiliate Insert).

## **4\. Ingredient Sourcing & Substitution Rules**

* **The "Pasar" Rule:** 100% of the ingredients must be easily found in an average Indonesian traditional market (*pasar tradisional*) or local convenience store (*minimarket*).  
* **Mandatory Substitutions:** If an inherently healthy but slightly premium ingredient is used (e.g., Chia seeds, Olive oil, Honey), the AI must mentally verify that it is widely available online or in standard Indonesian supermarkets, and not exclusively in high-end grocery stores.  
* **Cost Efficiency:** Always lean towards the most affordable protein sources (Tofu, Tempeh, Eggs, Chicken Breast, local fish like *Ikan Lele* or *Ikan Nila*) rather than expensive meats (Salmon, Wagyu, Ribeye).

## **5\. Forbidden Culinary Elements**

The AI must NEVER generate recipes containing:

* Rare imported berries (e.g., Raspberries, Blackberries) \-\> *Substitute with local fruits like Banana, Papaya, Mango, or Dragon Fruit.*  
* Expensive garnishes (e.g., Truffle oil, Edible gold, Saffron, Caviar).  
* Complex French culinary techniques (e.g., Sous-vide, flambé, complex reductions) that require high skill or specialized tools.
