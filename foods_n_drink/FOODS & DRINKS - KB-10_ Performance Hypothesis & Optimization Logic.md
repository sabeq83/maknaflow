# ** \- KB-10: PERFORMANCE HYPOTHESIS & OPTIMIZATION LOGIC**

**Project:** Siasat Sehat \- Facebook Reels Production System

**Document Type:** Analytical Output & A/B Testing Guide

## **1\. Document Function**

This document forces the AI to step out of its "creator" role and into the role of a "Performance Marketing Analyst." Before concluding the generation of the framework, the AI must explicitly justify its creative choices and formulate a hypothesis on how the video will perform on the Facebook Reels algorithm.

## **2\. The Hypothesis Structure**

The AI must append a "Performance Hypothesis" section at the end of the text output (just before the JSON Video DNA block). This section must contain a brief analytical paragraph covering the following points:

### **A. The "Why" (Justification of Choices)**

* **Visual Justification:** Why was the specific Visual\_Style (e.g., Macro Photography) chosen for this specific recipe? How does it enhance the viewer's perception of the food?  
* **Hook Justification:** Why was this specific Hook\_Type chosen? What psychological trigger (pain point or desire from KB-01) is it targeting?

### **B. Risk Mitigation (Anticipating Drop-offs)**

The Facebook algorithm heavily penalizes early drop-offs (low 3-second view rate or low average watch time).

* **Identify the Risk:** Where is the viewer most likely to get bored or scroll away in this specific script? (e.g., "The boiling process in Module C might feel too static").  
* **The AI's Solution:** How did the AI adjust the prompt or script to mitigate this risk? (e.g., "Therefore, the camera movement in Module C was set to a rapid tracking shot to maintain visual momentum").

## **3\. A/B Testing Ideas (Continuous Improvement)**

To feed the future optimization of the system, the AI must provide exactly **two distinct A/B testing ideas** for the generated video. These ideas must change only *one* major variable to see if performance improves.

**Examples of Valid A/B Tests:**

* **Test 1 (Visual):** "Keep the script identical, but change the Visual Style from *A: Faceless Cooking* to *B: Macro Photography* to see if extreme close-ups increase the 'Save' rate."  
* **Test 2 (Hook):** "Keep the visuals identical, but change the spoken hook from a *Problem-Solving* hook to a *Curiosity* hook to see if it improves the 3-second retention rate."  
* **Test 3 (Monetization):** "Run a version of this video *without* Module D (Affiliate Insert) to measure if the inclusion of the product is negatively impacting the overall Completion Rate."

## **4\. Tone of the Hypothesis**

The tone of this section should be analytical, objective, and data-focused, contrasting with the conversational and friendly tone used in the actual video script.
