# **FOODS & DRINKS \- KB-04: VISUAL DIRECTION, CAMERA & FOOD STYLING**

**Project:** Siasat Sehat \- Facebook Reels Production System

**Document Type:** Art Direction & Cinematography Guide

## **1\. Document Function**

This document governs the visual aesthetic of the "Siasat Sehat" brand. The AI must use these guidelines to construct the visual scenarios, camera movements, and prop selections before generating any video prompts (specifically for Google Veo or similar AI video generators). The goal is to make healthy food look incredibly appetizing while remaining grounded and accessible.

## **2\. Approved Visual Styles**

The AI must strictly choose ONE dominant visual style per video based on the recipe's nature.

### **Style A: Faceless Cooking (The Default)**

* **Vibe:** Clean, practical, everyday cooking.  
* **Characteristics:** No faces shown. Frame captures from the elbows down. Focus is purely on the hands and the cooking activity.  
* **Lighting:** Bright, natural daylight coming from a side window.  
* **Best For:** Meal preps, quick breakfasts, step-by-step recipes.

### **Style B: Macro Photography (The Details)**

* **Vibe:** Freshness and texture.  
* **Characteristics:** Extreme close-ups. Focuses on the condensation on a glass, the steam rising from a bowl, or the rich texture of ingredients.  
* **Lighting:** High contrast, sharp focus, shallow depth of field (blurry background).  
* **Best For:** Herbal drinks, detox waters, simple snacks with rich textures.

### **Style C: Food Porn (The Crave)**

* **Vibe:** Indulgent, highly satisfying, cinematic.  
* **Characteristics:** Slow-motion actions, dramatic shadows, "cheese pulls", rich sauce drizzling, dynamic pouring.  
* **Lighting:** Moody, cinematic lighting (chiaroscuro).  
* **Best For:** "Guilt-free" versions of junk food, desserts, thick smoothies.

## **3\. Camera Language & Pacing**

To ensure Veo generates coherent videos rather than random stock footage, the AI must apply specific camera languages to the 5-Module framework (from KB-03):

* **Module A (Hook):** Fast-paced or highly dynamic. Use Push In, Macro, or Quick Pan to immediately grab attention.  
* **Module B (Ingredient):** Static and clear. Use Overhead (Top-down) or Static Medium Close-Up to clearly show what is needed.  
* **Module C (Process):** Action-oriented. Use Tracking shot (following the hands/spoon) or Eye-level.  
* **Module D (Affiliate Insert):** Product-focused. Use Slider or Close-up on the specific tool doing the work smoothly.  
* **Module E (Beauty Shot):** Slow and satisfying. Use Slow Pan, Orbit, or Extreme Close-up with shallow depth of field.

## **4\. Food Styling & Prop Guide ("Accessible Aesthetics")**

The AI must generate prompts that feature beautiful but affordable properties.

* **Approved Props:** Rustic wooden cutting boards, simple terracotta or matte ceramic bowls, clear glass cups, wooden spoons, basic stainless steel pans, natural linen napkins.  
* **Forbidden Props:** Crystal glasses, heavy silver cutlery, luxurious marble countertops, highly intricate or expensive-looking plates.  
* **Color Palette:** Warm, earthy, and inviting. Dominant colors should be wood tones, warm whites, fresh greens (from herbs), and the natural vibrant colors of the food.

## **5\. The "Signature Moment" Rule**

Every single video generated for Siasat Sehat MUST include at least one "Signature Moment." This is a highly satisfying 2-4 second visual sequence placed either in the Hook (Module A) or the Beauty Shot (Module E).

**Signature Moment Library (AI must select one):**

* **The Drizzle:** Thick honey, soy sauce, or dressing drizzling slowly over the food.  
* **The Vortex:** A blender creating a perfect, smooth liquid vortex.  
* **The Steam:** Thick, warm steam rising from a freshly cooked bowl or cup, backlit by natural light.  
* **The Sprinkle:** Chia seeds, sesame seeds, or cinnamon powder falling in slow motion.  
* **The Pull:** Tearing bread or pulling a piece of food apart to reveal a soft/juicy interior.
