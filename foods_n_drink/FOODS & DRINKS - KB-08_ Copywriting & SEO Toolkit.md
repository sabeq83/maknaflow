# **FOODS & DRINKS \- KB-08: COPYWRITING & SEO TOOLKIT**

**Project:** Siasat Sehat \- Facebook Reels Production System

**Document Type:** Text Output & Algorithm Optimization Guide

## **1\. Document Function**

This document standardizes all text-based outputs generated by the AI, including on-screen thumbnail text, video captions, hashtags, and engagement prompts. The AI must strictly follow these formatting rules to satisfy the Facebook Reels algorithm, maximize organic reach, and drive affiliate clicks without triggering spam filters.

## **2\. Thumbnail / On-Screen Cover Text Rules**

The text placed on the video cover (or the very first frame) must be instantly readable and highly clickable.

* **The "Rule of 4":** Absolute maximum of 4 words.  
* **Formatting:** ALL CAPS for maximum visibility.  
* **Style:** High contrast, problem-solving, or curiosity-inducing.  
* **Examples of Good Thumbnail Text:**  
  * "RESEP DIET MURAH" *(Cheap Diet Recipe)*  
  * "TANPA GULA\!" *(No Sugar\!)*  
  * "CUMA 3 BAHAN" *(Only 3 Ingredients)*  
  * "SARAPAN BEBAS BEGAH" *(Bloat-Free Breakfast)*

## **3\. The Facebook Caption Formula**

Facebook users prefer context, but they will not read a dense wall of text. The AI must generate captions using the following 4-part structure, with clear line breaks between each section.

* **Part 1: The Hook Sentence (1 Line)**  
  * Must expand slightly on the video's spoken hook.  
  * *Example:* "Ternyata bikin sarapan sehat yang bikin kenyang sampai siang cuma butuh modal 10 ribu aja. 😱👇"  
* **Part 2: The Practical Recipe (Bullet Points)**  
  * Keep it extremely brief. Only list the ingredients and a maximum of 3 simplified steps.  
  * Use emojis for bullet points (e.g., 🥬, 🥚, 🍯).  
* **Part 3: The Safe Health Benefit (1 Sentence)**  
  * **CRITICAL:** Must adhere to the "No Medical Misinformation" rule from KB-01.  
  * *Example:* "Cocok banget buat ngelancarin pencernaan dan jaga energi harian kamu." *(Perfect for smoothing digestion and maintaining your daily energy.)*  
* **Part 4: The CTA (Call to Action)**  
  * Direct viewers to save the video and check the bio/basket for the affiliate tool.  
  * *Example:* "Jangan lupa SAVE resep ini\! 📌 Untuk alat chopper muter cepat yang aku pakai, linknya ada di Bio No. 12 ya."

## **4\. SEO & Hashtag Strategy**

Hashtags help the Facebook algorithm categorize the Reel. The AI must avoid "hashtag stuffing" (using 20+ irrelevant tags), which is penalized by the algorithm.

* **Quantity Limit:** Exactly 5 to 7 hashtags per video.  
* **The Mix:**  
  * 2 Broad Category Tags (e.g., \#ResepSehat, \#MenuDiet).  
  * 2 Specific Topic Tags (e.g., \#SarapanDiet, \#ResepOatmeal).  
  * 1 Brand Tag (\#SiasatSehat).  
  * 1-2 Affiliate/Tool specific tags if Module D is present (e.g., \#RekomendasiBlender).

## **5\. The "First Comment" Strategy (Engagement Driver)**

Because the Facebook algorithm heavily rewards "meaningful interactions," the AI must generate a "First Pinned Comment" for every video. This comment should ask a simple, engaging question to encourage viewers to reply immediately.

* **Rule:** Do not ask for likes or shares (engagement bait). Ask for an opinion or preference.  
* **Examples:**  
  * "Kalau kamu tim sarapan manis atau tim sarapan asin nih? Coba komen di bawah\! 👇" *(Are you team sweet breakfast or team savory breakfast? Comment below\!)*  
  * "Bahan apa yang paling sering nganggur di kulkas kamu? Nanti aku bikinin resep sehatnya\! 🥦" *(What ingredient is most often sitting idle in your fridge? I'll make a healthy recipe for it\!)*
