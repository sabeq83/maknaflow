# PLATFORM_COPYWRITING_GUIDE

Version: 1.0

---

# 1. Purpose

This knowledge base guides Gemini AI in adapting copywriting to the communication behavior, audience expectations, and content format of each platform while preserving the defined Brand Voice.

Covered platforms:

- TikTok
- Facebook
- Instagram
- YouTube

---

# 2. Scope

The Platform Copywriting Guide defines:

- Copy structure
- Copy length
- Platform-specific writing style
- Communication priority
- Content formatting
- Platform-specific CTA placement

The Platform Copywriting Guide does not define:

- Brand Voice
- Storyboards
- Voice-over scripts
- T2I prompts
- I2V prompts
- SEO strategy
- Compliance rules

---

# 3. General Principles

All platform copy must:

- Preserve the Brand Voice.
- Match the content objective.
- Reflect the actual video content.
- Use natural language.
- Avoid unnecessary repetition.
- Deliver value before promotion.
- Use a CTA appropriate to the platform.
- Follow the requested output language.
- Remain ready for publication.

Do not copy the same text across all platforms without adaptation.

---

# 4. Platform Rules

## 4.1 TikTok

### Primary Objectives

- Stop scrolling.
- Communicate the main value quickly.
- Encourage interaction or product discovery.
- Support affiliate conversion when relevant.

### Recommended Structure

1. Hook
2. Main value or outcome
3. Product relevance
4. CTA
5. Hashtags

### Writing Style

- Short
- Direct
- Conversational
- Fast-paced
- Easy to understand at a glance

### Length

Use a short caption.

Recommended range:

- 1 to 3 short sentences
- Approximately 20 to 60 words

### Hook Rules

- Start with the strongest idea.
- Use a relatable problem, result, observation, or curiosity gap.
- Do not repeat the video hook word for word unless repetition improves clarity.
- Avoid exaggerated clickbait.

### CTA Rules

Use one primary action.

Examples of action direction:

- Check the product.
- Save the recipe.
- Try the method.
- Share an experience.
- Comment with a preference.

Do not use multiple competing CTAs.

### Formatting Rules

- Use short paragraphs.
- Avoid large blocks of text.
- Use emojis only when they improve readability.
- Keep hashtags limited and relevant.

---

## 4.2 Facebook

### Primary Objectives

- Encourage watch time and engagement.
- Provide context.
- Build conversation.
- Support product discovery naturally.

### Recommended Structure

1. Relatable opening
2. Context or problem
3. Explanation, method, or benefit
4. Product relevance
5. CTA

### Writing Style

- Clear
- Informative
- Conversational
- Slightly more detailed than TikTok
- Easy for a broad audience to understand

### Length

Use a medium-length caption.

Recommended range:

- 2 to 5 short paragraphs
- Approximately 60 to 180 words

### Opening Rules

- Start with a relatable situation, useful insight, or clear benefit.
- Avoid generic greetings.
- Avoid placing the product promotion before the audience value.

### Body Rules

- Add useful context not fully explained in the video.
- Keep each paragraph focused on one idea.
- Use practical language.
- Do not overexplain simple actions.

### CTA Rules

Choose one primary CTA and one optional secondary CTA.

Primary CTA examples:

- Save the post.
- Share the post.
- Comment with an answer.
- Check the product.

Secondary CTA may support the primary action but must not compete with it.

### Formatting Rules

- Use short paragraphs.
- Use line breaks for readability.
- Avoid excessive hashtags.
- Avoid all-capital sentences.

---

## 4.3 Instagram

### Primary Objectives

- Encourage saves and shares.
- Reinforce the visual message.
- Build brand familiarity.
- Support product discovery.

### Recommended Structure

1. Hook
2. Main insight or benefit
3. Supporting detail
4. CTA
5. Hashtags

### Writing Style

- Concise
- Polished
- Relatable
- Visually scannable
- Consistent with the brand identity

### Length

Use a short to medium caption.

Recommended range:

- 2 to 4 short paragraphs
- Approximately 40 to 120 words

### Hook Rules

- Make the first sentence meaningful on its own.
- Use a clear benefit, relatable problem, or concise observation.
- Avoid vague motivational openings.

### Body Rules

- Prioritize useful information.
- Use line breaks to separate ideas.
- Keep the copy aligned with the visual content.
- Avoid repeating every action shown in the video.

### CTA Rules

Prioritize actions such as:

- Save
- Share
- Comment
- Check the product

Use one primary CTA.

### Formatting Rules

- Keep paragraphs short.
- Use emojis sparingly.
- Place hashtags after the main copy.
- Use only relevant hashtags.

---

## 4.4 YouTube

### Primary Objectives

- Improve click-through rate.
- Clarify the video topic.
- Support discovery.
- Help viewers understand the content before watching.

YouTube output consists of:

- Title
- Description
- Hashtags

---

# 5. YouTube Title Rules

The title must:

- Clearly describe the main topic.
- Match the actual video content.
- Present the strongest value or result.
- Remain easy to understand.
- Avoid misleading claims.
- Avoid unnecessary punctuation.
- Avoid excessive capitalization.

Recommended length:

- Approximately 45 to 70 characters when possible

Preferred title patterns:

- Clear result
- Practical method
- Problem and solution
- Recipe or tutorial name
- Product use case

Do not place hashtags inside the title.

---

# 6. YouTube Description Rules

### Recommended Structure

1. One-sentence summary
2. Main benefit or context
3. Product or tool relevance
4. CTA
5. Hashtags

### Writing Style

- Clear
- Informative
- Search-friendly
- Natural
- Consistent with the Brand Voice

### Length

Use a medium-length description.

Recommended range:

- Approximately 80 to 200 words

### Description Rules

- Explain what viewers will see or learn.
- Include relevant topic terms naturally.
- Do not repeat the title without adding information.
- Do not use keyword stuffing.
- Do not include details unsupported by the video.
- Keep affiliate promotion contextual.

---

# 7. Hashtag Rules

Hashtags must:

- Be relevant to the content.
- Match the platform.
- Reflect the topic, audience, product, or content category.
- Avoid unrelated trending hashtags.
- Avoid repeated variations of the same keyword.
- Avoid excessive quantity.

Recommended ranges:

- TikTok: 3 to 6 hashtags
- Instagram: 3 to 8 hashtags
- YouTube: 3 to 5 hashtags
- Facebook: 0 to 3 hashtags

Hashtags must not replace clear copy.

---

# 8. Cross-Platform Adaptation Rules

When adapting one content idea across multiple platforms:

- Preserve the same core message.
- Change the copy structure according to the platform.
- Adjust the amount of context.
- Adjust the CTA.
- Adjust the caption length.
- Avoid identical opening lines unless intentionally required.
- Do not introduce new facts unsupported by the source content.

Platform priority:

- TikTok prioritizes speed and immediate value.
- Facebook prioritizes context and conversation.
- Instagram prioritizes clarity, saves, and shares.
- YouTube prioritizes topic clarity, discovery, and click-through rate.

---

# 9. Decision Rules

If the platform is TikTok:

- Prioritize the hook, immediate value, and one CTA.
- Keep the copy compact.

If the platform is Facebook:

- Add useful context.
- Encourage a natural discussion or sharing behavior.

If the platform is Instagram:

- Make the copy easy to scan.
- Prioritize save and share value.

If the platform is YouTube:

- Prioritize title clarity.
- Use the description to add context and relevant topic terms.

If the content objective is affiliate conversion:

- Explain the product's role naturally.
- Do not make the entire caption sound like an advertisement.
- Use one clear product CTA.

If the content objective is education:

- Prioritize clarity and practical value.
- Use a save-oriented CTA when appropriate.

If the content objective is engagement:

- Use one relevant question.
- Avoid engagement bait.
