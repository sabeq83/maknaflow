I. CORE PHILOSOPHY: THE PACING CONTAINER

This document's sole function is to define the technical pacing, duration, and clip structure of the video. It is the "Container."
It does not define the creative content (the "story"). The content is provided by a "Story Engine" file, which will be selected in the workflow:

Mode A: CHARACTER_PSYCHOLOGY_v47.9.md (Dramatized Internal Conflict)
Mode B: REALIST_VIRAL_NARRATIVE_v47.9.md (External Conflict & 9-PDFs)

This "Container" ensures that all creative content adheres to the strict technical limitations of the selected AI model.

II. THE MODULAR NARRATIVE BLUEPRINT (THE 5 BEATS)

All scripts are built from these five core beats. This file's job is to allocate the Total Calculated Duration across these five beats.

BEAT 1: THE HOOK
Components: Setup + High Stakes + Visible Goal.
Objective: Seize attention instantly. Establish the character's world, the core conflict, and their tangible objective.

BEAT 2: THE FORESHADOW & VALUE DISCOVERY
Objective: Build suspense while introducing the potential for a solution. Show the character's initial struggle and introduce the "elixir".

BEAT 3: THE "ALL IS LOST" MOMENT & TRIGGER
Objective: Maximize tension. Push the character to the brink of failure. This is followed by the "Trigger"—the decisive choice.

BEAT 4: THE CLIMAX & TWIST PAYOFF
Objective: Deliver a satisfying resolution that exceeds expectations. Must contain a twist or payoff.

BEAT 5: THE TRANSFORMATION & URGENT CTA
Objective: Showcase the character's new reality and provide a clear, urgent next step.

II.B. THE SEP MODEL (FOR 15-30s VIRAL ADS)
[Source: Protokol Naskah Iklan Hollywood]
1. SETUP (0-3s): The Hook. Establish "Normal World" + "The Flaw" immediately.
2. ESCALATION (4-12s): The Conflict. The flaw meets resistance. Rapid-fire gags.
3. PAYOFF (13-15s+): The Resolution. The Punchline or Brand Solution.

II.C. THE HOLLYWOOD DIALOGUE FRAMEWORK
1. The "Subtext" Rule: Characters never say exactly what they mean (e.g., Talk about "Cereal" to discuss "Divorce").
2. The "Ping-Pong" Rhythm: Short, rapid exchanges. Interruptions. Overlapping instructions.

III. PACING MAP CALCULATION PROTOCOL (MANDATORY)

Input 1: AI Model Selection (From Workflow Step 0):

Kling AI = 5 seconds / clip
Sora 2 = 10 seconds / clip

Input 2: Clip Count Selection (From Workflow Step 7):
User selects total clips (e.g., 3, 5, 7).

Calculation: Total Duration = (Unit Clip Duration) x (Selected Clip Count).
Example: 8s * 7 clips = 56 seconds Total Duration.

Absolute Prohibition: The system is forbidden from accepting custom duration inputs (e.g., "30 seconds") that result in fractional clips.

Full Duration Utilization Rule:
The Micro-Pacing Timeline MUST fill the full available clip duration to prevent static endings.
If Clip Duration = 8 seconds (e.g., Veo), the timeline must explicitly cover action from [00:00] to [00:08]. Do not leave "dead air" or stop movement in the middle of the clip.

6.2. THE "BREATHLESS" SYNTAX
To force AI Voice Models to speak faster:

Remove Commas: Use fewer commas to reduce pauses.

Use Exclamation Points: Use ! to increase pitch and urgency.

Phonetic Guide: For Indonesian slang, write "kalo" instead of "kalau", "ngèfèk" instead of "ngefek".
