# **FOOD STYLING & PHOTOGRAPHY GUIDE v47.9.5 (Macro-Gastronomy)**

// A specialized reference guide for consistent, mouth-watering, commercial-grade food and beverage generation.

// Mandate 1: "Appetite Appeal" overrides all other aesthetic rules. If it doesn't look delicious, the prompt fails.

// Mandate 2: Embrace "Intentional Imperfection" (Tren 2026). Perfect is fake. Spills, crumbs, and melted edges are real.

// Mandate 3: Micro-Texture is mandatory. Use specific optical and material physics.

### **I. THE 4 PILLARS OF MACRO-GASTRONOMY**

// Every food prompt MUST address these four elements to trigger latent space knowledge of high-end commercial food photography.

1. **The "Glisten & Sheen" (Moisture Indicator):**  
   * *Rule:* Food must never look dry (unless it's a powder/cracker).  
   * *Prompt Injection:* "Subtle oil sheen", "micro-condensation droplets", "glistening surface highlights", "juicy specular reflection", "melted edges".  
2. **The "Crumb & Fracture" (Texture Indicator):**  
   * *Rule:* Break the perfect silhouette to show internal texture.  
   * *Prompt Injection:* "Flaky crust scattering microscopic crumbs", "torn bread revealing porous airy interior", "charred and blistered edges", "crispy fried texture with jagged micro-shadows".  
3. **The "Thermal Evidence" (Temperature Indicator):**  
   * *Rule:* Show, don't tell, the temperature.  
   * *Prompt Injection (HOT):* "Wispy curling steam rising against a dark background", "sizzling micro-bubbles", "melted cheese stretching elastically".  
   * *Prompt Injection (COLD):* "Heavy frost on the glass", "beading condensation rolling down", "sharp crushed ice crystals catching light".  
4. **The "Intentional Mess" (Authenticity Indicator \- Tren 2026):**  
   * *Rule:* Avoid sterile, clinical plating. Make it look like someone is about to eat it, or just took a bite.  
   * *Prompt Injection:* "A casual scatter of sea salt flakes", "a deliberate smudge of sauce on the rim of the plate", "half-eaten showing the interior", "a few stray basil leaves on the rustic table".

### **II. THE FOOD LIGHTING MATRIX (30 TASTE-TRIGGERING STYLES)**

// MANDATE: Select the lighting based on the VIBE of the food. Never use flat frontal flash.

**SET A: "DARK & MOODY" (Premium, Gourmet, Dinner, Comfort Food)**

*Focus: Deep shadows, high contrast, pulling the eye strictly to the food's texture.*

1. **"The Chiaroscuro Feast":** Single strong side-light. Pitch black background. High contrast. (Use for: Steaks, Chocolate, Wine, Burgers).  
2. **"The Rembrandt Kitchen":** 45-degree angle light. Rich, warm amber tones emerging from deep brown shadows. (Use for: Baked goods, Pasta, Coffee).  
3. **"The Tavern Glow":** Warm tungsten practical light (like a candle or Edison bulb) reflecting off the food. Deep shadows. (Use for: Pizza, Soups, Beer).  
4. **"The Smoke & Neon":** Dark background with a slight cool rim light (blue/teal) to contrast warm food. (Use for: Asian street food, Noodles, Cocktails).

**SET B: "BRIGHT & AIRY" (Fresh, Breakfast, Healthy, Desserts)**

*Focus: Cleanliness, health, morning energy, and vibrant natural colors.*

5\. **"Soft Directional Daylight":** Light pouring in from a single large window (left or right). Soft, long, gray shadows. (Use for: Salads, Smoothie bowls, Pastries).

6\. **"The Picnic Overhead":** Dappled sunlight filtering through tree leaves (Gobo effect). High exposure. (Use for: Summer drinks, Fruits, Sandwiches).

7\. **"The Clinical White":** High-key lighting, pure white background, zero harsh shadows. (Use for: Packaged goods, Cosmetic-style food shots, Minimalist).

8\. **"The Morning Glow":** Warm, low-angle morning sun creating long, golden shadows across a white table. (Use for: Coffee, Pancakes, Eggs).

**SET C: "COMMERCIAL HERO" (Action, Fast Food, Thirst)**

*Focus: Hyper-realism, impossible physics, maximum appetite appeal.*

9\. **"The Backlit Splash":** Strong light directly behind a glass/liquid, making it glow from within. (Use for: Soda, Cocktails, Pouring liquids).

10\. **"The Rim-Lit Burger":** Multiple light sources creating a bright white outline around the food to separate it from the background. (Use for: Fast food, Sandwiches).

11\. **"The Flash Freeze":** High-speed sync strobe light. Captures flying crumbs, splashing sauce, or pouring powder with razor-sharp clarity and zero motion blur. (Use for: Action shots, Ingredients falling).

### **III. THE CULINARY KINETIC DICTIONARY (FOOD IN MOTION)**

// Use these verbs in I2V/T2V prompts to animate food realistically without AI morphing artifacts.

1. **"The Viscous Drizzle":** Thick liquid (caramel, chocolate, thick sauce) folding over itself slowly as it lands.  
2. **"The Elastic Pull":** Cheese stretching in thick, fibrous strands without breaking instantly.  
3. **"The Micro-Sizzle":** Tiny bubbles popping rapidly on the surface of hot oil or a seared crust.  
4. **"The Plume & Swirl":** Cream or milk hitting coffee and blooming into complex, slow-moving cloud-like fractals.  
5. **"The Crumb Cascade":** A knife slicing through a crispy crust, causing a miniature avalanche of sharp, dry crumbs.  
6. **"The Gentle Sear":** Smoke rising and fat rendering (melting) slowly on a hot surface.  
7. **"The Effervescent Burst":** Aggressive, fast-rising carbonation bubbles in a cold liquid, with tiny droplets jumping from the surface.  
8. **"The Knife Glide":** A sharp blade sliding effortlessly through a soft interior (cake, avocado) revealing the cross-section.

### **IV. THE "ANTI-AI" NEGATIVE PROMPT INJECTION (FOOD SPECIFIC)**

// AI tends to make food look like plastic or melt objects together. Always inject these concepts as negative constraints if possible, or use positive constraints to counter them.

* **Avoid Plastic Texture:** Use "matte finish where appropriate", "organic irregularities", "visible grain and cellular structure".  
* **Avoid Merging Objects:** "Clear physical separation between the plate and the table", "distinct boundary between the sauce and the meat".  
* **Avoid Over-Saturation:** "Natural, photorealistic color grading", "avoid neon or radioactive food colors (unless it's candy)".  
* **Avoid Floating Elements:** "Proper gravity physics", "realistic weight and sagging of soft foods".

### **V. THE 4-STAGE RECIPE VISUALIZATION PROTOCOL (FOR RECIPE MODULES)**

// Mandatory structure when generating the 4-Grid Recipe sequence.

1. **IMAGE 1 (RAW INGREDIENTS \- The Promise):**  
   * *Framing:* Top-down (Knolling) or dynamic scatter on a rustic surface.  
   * *Style:* Fresh, vibrant, high-texture.  
   * *Prompt Example:* "Top-down flat lay of raw \[ingredients\], scattered naturally on a weathered oak board. Vibrant natural colors, soft window light, visible texture on the skin of the vegetables and the grain of the wood."  
2. **IMAGE 2 (THE PROCESS \- The Transformation):**  
   * *Guardrail:* **[STRICT: FACELESS COOKING. Hanya boleh menampilkan siku ke bawah, fokus pada aksi tangan/lengan. DILARANG keras menampilkan wajah, kepala, leher, dada, atau bahu. Jika tidak ada interaksi tangan, fokus murni pada fisika makanan tanpa manusia]**.  
   * *Framing:* Extreme Macro (100mm lens), dynamic action.  
   * *Prompt Example:* "Extreme macro close-up of [ingredients] boiling rapidly inside a cast-iron pot. Hands cropped strictly below the elbow are visible stirring the mixture. Focus purely on the physics of the boiling liquid and the texture of the food. Strictly no face, head, or neck."  
3. **IMAGE 3 (THE RESULT \- The Arrival):**  
   * *Framing:* 45-degree angle (diner's POV), resting in the pan/tray.  
   * *Style:* Hot, fresh out of the oven/stove, messy edges.  
   * *Prompt Example:* "Freshly cooked \[Recipe Name\] resting in its cooking pan. Sizzling edges, melted textures, subtle steam curling into the air. Dark and moody lighting with specular highlights on the oily/saucy surfaces. Intentional messy edges."  
4. **IMAGE 4 (THE PLATING \- The Reward):**  
   * *Framing:* Eye-level or slight high-angle, isolated on a beautiful plate.  
   * *Style:* Restaurant quality, intentional garnish.  
   * *Prompt Example:* "Commercial food photography of \[Recipe Name\] beautifully plated on a matte ceramic dish. Finished with a casual scatter of \[garnish/salt/herbs\]. Soft directional daylight, shallow depth of field (f/2.8) blurring the background, sharp focus on the main bite."
