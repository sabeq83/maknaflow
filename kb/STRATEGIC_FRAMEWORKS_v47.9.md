I. THE INTEGRATED CEP MASTERMIND WORKFLOW (THEORY)
This is the primary workflow that unifies all strategic frameworks into one integrated process. The goal is to build Mental Availability by systematically linking the brand to the most relevant Category Entry Points (CEPs).
A new, mandatory component (v47.9) is the "Hot Trend & Cultural Context Analysis." This data is retrieved during Step 2 of the workflow and serves as a critical filter for all dynamic creative generation.

II. STAGE 1: THE CAVAC MODEL CORE (THEORY)
(Source: CAVAC Model.docx & CAVAC 2.0.docx)
The core formula is CEP × Advertised Value × Angle = Content. The process begins by dissecting the purchase situation (CEP).

2.1. 6 CEP Types (Category Entry Points)

| CEP Type | Definition | Example CEPs |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Problem-Solution Based** | Decision occurs due to a problem that must be solved. | "When experiencing discomfort, when an obstacle appears." |
| **2. Routine-Based** | Decision occurs as part of an automatic, low-thought routine. | "When starting the day, when doing a daily chore." |
| **3. Commitment-Based** | Decision is a habit but requires more effort to maintain. | "When performing a recurring step that requires dedication." |
| **4. Aspirational-Based** | Decision occurs from a desire to reach an ideal state. | "When wanting to improve quality of life, achieve optimal results." |
| **5. Opportunistic-Based** | Decision occurs due to an unplanned external trigger. | "When seeing a promotion/discount, getting a recommendation." |
| **6. Emotional-Based** | Decision occurs from an internal emotional drive. | "When stressed and wanting to feel better, wanting to self-gift." |

2.2. Ws Framework for Defining Sub-CEPs (THEORY)
Each CEP type is broken down into specific Sub-CEPs using the "Ws" framework:
* **When?** (Time)
* **Where?** (Location)
* **With/For Whom?** (Social context)
* **How Feeling?** (Emotional state)
* **While?** (Concurrent activity)
* **With What?** (Companion objects)
* **Why?** (Goal)

III. STAGE 2: ADVERTISED VALUE (VFO FRAMEWORK) (THEORY)
(Source: VFO Framework Cool.docx)
Formula: VFO = Value = Feature × Outcome

| Value Type | Basis | Example | Role in Marketing |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Concrete** | Tangible proof | "5000 mAh Battery, 99.9% Uptime" | Rational, sales pitch, objective proof. |
| **Instinctive** | Sensory/emotional | "Fresh taste, instant moisturized skin" | Fast emotional trigger. |
| **Uncharted** | Education/innovation | "AI analytics, microcapsule tech" | Differentiation, USP, needs education. |
| **Aspirational** | Identity/dreams | "Nike = courage, Apple = creativity" | Emotional bond, social status. |

IV. STAGE 3: INTENT DEFINITION (CAVAC 2.0) (THEORY)
(Source: CAVAC 2.0.docx)

4.1. Selling Intent
* **No Sell:** Brand-related, no benefit, no CTA. (Top-funnel).
* **Soft Sell:** Mentions brand, shows benefit, no hard CTA. (Consideration).
* **Hard Sell:** Mentions brand + explicit CTA (price, promo, urgency). (Conversion).

4.2. Communication Intent
* **Educate:** Provide new understanding.
* **Trigger:** Nudge fear, urgency, or worry.
* **Inspire:** Lift aspirations & hope.
* **Clarify:** Correct a misunderstanding.
* **Validate:** Confirm the audience's choice is correct.
* **Entertain:** Amuse, lighthearted, make them smile.
* **Proof:** Show objective, tangible evidence.
* **Call to Act:** Explicit/implicit call to action.
* **Reinforce:** Strengthen the belief of existing users.

V. STAGE 4: THE GOAL - BRAND SALIENCE (THEORY)
(Source: Brand Salience.docx)
* **Definition:** Brand Salience is the probability of a brand to come to mind by a buyer in a purchasing situation (a CEP).
* **Memory Structures:** Salience is built through the quantity and quality of memory structures a buyer has about a brand.

VI. STAGE 5: THE AMPLIFIER - DISTINCTIVE BRAND ASSETS (DBAs) (THEORY)
(Source: Ipsos Views & BAT Guide To DBAs)

6.1. Measuring DBAs
* **Fame:** How many consumers recognize the asset.
* **Uniqueness:** How exclusively the asset is linked to only one specific brand.

6.2. Asset Classification (BAT Grid)
| Fame | Uniqueness | Asset Category | Strategic Implication |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| High | High | **Strong Asset** | Effective -> maximum exploitation. |
| High | Low | **Shared Asset** | Vulnerable -> Needs differentiation. |
| Low | High | **Emerging Asset** | Potential -> Needs consistency. |
| Low | Low | **Weak Asset** | Irrelevant -> Eliminate or redesign. |

VII. STAGE 6: EXECUTION STYLE - SHOPPERTAINMENT (THEORY)
(Source: SEA Shoppertainment 2024.docx)
* **Concept:** Shoppertainment = Shopping + Entertainment.
* **Journey:** Entertain (Fun content) -> Engage (Live shopping, collabs) -> Convert (Frictionless purchase).

VIII. STAGE 7: B2B ADAPTATION (THEORY)

XI. BEHAVIORAL PSYCHOLOGY INJECTION (THE PERSUASION LAYER)
[Source: Protokol Prompting Hibrida]

11.1. SYSTEM 1 vs. SYSTEM 2 TARGETING (Kahneman)
* **IF Goal = Awareness/Viral:** Target SYSTEM 1 (Fast, Emotional, Instinctive). Use high-contrast visuals, facial close-ups, shock, humor.
* **IF Goal = Conversion/B2B:** Target SYSTEM 2 (Slow, Logical, Calculating). Use data overlay, clear comparisons, expert testimonials.

11.2. THE 6 PRINCIPLES OF PERSUASION (Cialdini) - VISUAL VISUALIZATION
1.  **Reciprocity:** Visual of gifting, sharing secrets, or "free value".
2.  **Scarcity:** Visual countdowns, emptying shelves, fading light, "last chance".
3.  **Authority:** Uniforms, podiums, low-angle camera, books/certificates.
4.  **Consistency:** Characters making a public commitment.
5.  **Liking:** Attractive lighting, genuine smiles, mirroring.
6.  **Consensus (Social Proof):** Crowd shots, UGC style, multiple people nodding.
