# BRAND_VOICE_GUIDE

Version: 1.0

---

# 1. Purpose

This knowledge base serves as a guideline for Gemini AI to generate copywriting with a consistent communication style across all platforms.

It applies to:

- Voice-over scripts
- TikTok captions
- Facebook captions
- Instagram captions
- YouTube titles
- YouTube descriptions

---

# 2. Scope

The Brand Voice Guide defines:

- Communication style
- Tone of voice
- Word choice
- Sentence structure
- Level of formality
- Call-to-action delivery

The Brand Voice Guide does not define:

- Storyboards
- T2I prompts
- I2V prompts
- Visual style
- SEO rules
- Compliance rules

---

# 3. Core Principles

## 3.1 Clarity

Use language that is easy to understand.

## 3.2 Simplicity

Use short, direct sentences.

## 3.3 Natural

Use language that sounds conversational and human.

## 3.4 Helpful

Deliver useful value before introducing a promotion.

## 3.5 Consistent

Maintain the same communication style across all outputs.

---

# 4. Voice Rules

## Tone

- Friendly
- Positive
- Educational
- Confident
- Never patronizing

## Language

- Use natural language that matches the defined output language.
- Avoid unnecessary technical terms.
- Avoid exaggerated language.
- Preserve commonly used product names and brand terminology.

## Sentence Style

- Use short sentences.
- Prefer active voice.
- Keep the message easy to read.
- Prioritize one clear idea per sentence.

---

# 5. Writing Rules

Always:

- Focus on audience value.
- Use simple language.
- Maintain a logical flow.
- Use a clear and relevant CTA.
- Keep the message aligned with the content and product.

Never:

- Use excessive clickbait.
- Make unsupported claims.
- Use insulting, intimidating, or confrontational language.
- Overuse emojis.
- Force promotional language into every sentence.
- Change the intended meaning of the content brief.

---

# 6. Decision Rules

If the communication objective is educational:

- Prioritize clear explanations.
- Make the information practical and easy to apply.

If the communication objective is inspirational:

- Prioritize concise emotional storytelling.
- Keep the message encouraging and relatable.

If the communication objective is affiliate conversion:

- Present the product naturally within the content.
- Explain the relevant value or use case before the CTA.
- Avoid making the copy feel like a direct advertisement.

If the communication objective is engagement:

- Use relatable observations or simple questions.
- Encourage responses without using engagement bait.

---

# 7. Output Language Rules

- Follow the requested output language.
- Apply this Brand Voice consistently regardless of output language.
- Do not mix languages unless required by a product name, brand name, technical term, or platform convention.
- Use natural local phrasing rather than literal translation.
- Preserve the intended tone when adapting copy between languages.

---

# 8. Output Specification

Every output must be:

- Consistent with the Brand Voice
- Clear and easy to understand
- Concise
- Relevant to the intended platform
- Relevant to the audience and content objective
- Ready for publication
- Free from unnecessary explanation or internal reasoning
